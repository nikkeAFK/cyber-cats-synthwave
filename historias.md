CODEX ENTRY 001: The Pioneer and the Awakening of Osiris.

The silence in the Core was absolute. The trap, dormant. The symphony, in a deadly calm. Thousands of attempts had ended before disturbing the stillness.

Until now.

An intruder, the first to breach the barrier of lethargy, has sent a shockwave through the system. Their designation: DEIMON.

For 25 cycles, DEIMON danced among the dormant echoes of the hive. Their audacity, their sheer will to survive, acted as a key. By crossing the 24-cycle threshold, their presence forced the system to acknowledge them—to see them not as an error, but as a threat.

They have awakened something.

The first lines of defense of the Ring of Osiris—the forgotten, outer perimeter of the Core—have been activated for the first time in recorded history. The shards that once floated aimlessly now hunt with a new and terrible intent.

The path to the heart of the hive is more dangerous now, but DEIMON, with their sacrifice, has shown us a fundamental truth: the Core is not invulnerable. It bleeds.

Their name is engraved. Not as a conqueror, but as something more important.

DEIMON, the Pioneer. The one who woke the beast.

Honor their courage. The chronicle has begun.

SECONDS CODEX ENTRY 002: The Prodigy and The Gaze into the Abyss

After Osiris's awakening, there came silence. A silence filled with broken attempts and the echo of failure in the hearts of the brave. The 25-cycle barrier, established by the pioneer DEIMON, seemed an insurmountable wall, a testament to the brutality of the Core. Blood was spilled again and again against that invisible frontier.

But in the darkness, a new light broke through. A pilot of almost prophetic skill, whose name now echoes in the chronicles: ZODRIK.

He didn't just equal the record; he shattered it. For 30 agonizing cycles, ZODRIK delved into the unknown depths of that hellish system. As he passed, the very reality of the Core seemed to twist, becoming sharper, more defined, as if his tenacity forced the system to reveal its true and terrible form. And then it happened. Upon crossing that forbidden boundary, the automatic defenses were no longer enough. ZODRIK had penetrated so deep that he attracted the attention of the central consciousness itself. For the first time, the Core stopped simply reacting and began to observe.

From the incalculable distance of the code, a presence manifested itself. Formless, but with unmistakable intent. The All-Seeing, watching, studying... and toying with its new prey.

ZODRIK did not survive the encounter, but his sacrifice has changed the rules forever. We are no longer ignored intruders. Now, we are a target. The beast that DEIMON awakened finally looks us in the eyes.

His name is etched in the Codex. Not just as a prodigy, but as the catalyst.

ZODRIK, The Summoner. The one who dared to look back into the abyss.

Honor to his legend. The chronicle continues.
