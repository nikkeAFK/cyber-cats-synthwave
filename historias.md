CODEX ENTRY 001: The Pioneer and the Awakening of Osiris
The silence in the Core was absolute. The trap, dormant. The symphony, in a deadly calm. Thousands of attempts had ended before disturbing the stillness.

Until now.

An intruder, the first to breach the barrier of lethargy, has sent a shockwave through the system. Their designation: DEIMON.

For 25 cycles, DEIMON danced among the dormant echoes of the hive. Their audacity, their sheer will to survive, acted as a key. By crossing the 24-cycle threshold, their presence forced the system to acknowledge them—to see them not as an error, but as a threat.

They have awakened something.

The first lines of defense of the Ring of Osiris—the forgotten, outer perimeter of the Core—have been activated for the first time in recorded history. The shards that once floated aimlessly now hunt with a new and terrible intent.

The path to the heart of the hive is more dangerous now, but DEIMON, with their sacrifice, has shown us a fundamental truth: the Core is not invulnerable. It bleeds.

Their name is engraved. Not as a conqueror, but as something more important.

DEIMON, the Pioneer. The one who woke the beast.

Honor their courage. The chronicle has begun.
