Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2025

At CYBERCATS-SYNTHWAVE, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle the information generated when you play and visit our page.


1. Information We Collect

When you play CYBERCATS-SYNTHWAVE or visit our website, we may collect the following information:

  • **Player Scores and Names:** If you surpass the weekly high score, you can enter a name. This information (the name and score) is stored in our database to be displayed on the global leaderboard within the game. **It is not associated with any other personal information, such as your IP address or device data.**
  • **Website Usage Data (Google Analytics):** We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our page. Google Analytics collects anonymous data such as the time you spend on the site, the pages you visit, and the type of device you use.
  • **Advertising Data (Google AdSense):** When your page is approved and displays AdSense ads, Google may collect anonymous data about ad interactions to show more relevant advertising. This is done directly by Google, and we do not store it.

**We do not collect or store IP addresses, email addresses, login data, or any other information that directly identifies you.**


2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for:

  • **Game Functionality:** The scores and names you provide are used exclusively for the global leaderboard within the game.
  • **Experience Improvement:** Anonymous data from Google Analytics helps us improve our website's design and performance.
  • **Monetization:** AdSense information is used by Google to display ads that allow us to generate revenue and continue developing games.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide us, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Scores and names are the only things we store, and we do so for the sole purpose of the leaderboard function.


4. Links to Other Websites

Our site may contain links to other websites that we do not operate (such as Pixabay for music or PayPal for donations). We have no control over their privacy policies. We recommend you review the privacy policies of every site you visit.


5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will let you know by posting the new version here. We recommend you check this page from time to time.


6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us.
